# Image credits - herb photos

The 480x480 JPEGs in this folder are the photos shown in the circular area of each
"Herbs you can actually name" card (`image` in `src/data/content.js`, rendered in
`src/sections/Ingredients.jsx`). Source: Wikimedia Commons. Nothing is changed except
a centre crop to a square and a resize.

**CC BY** and **CC BY-SA** photos require the credit to stay visible while they are
used; **CC0** and **public domain** photos need nothing.

## Credit line

While any of the CC BY / CC BY-SA photos are on the site, keep a line like this
somewhere a visitor can see it (footer or a small credits page):

> Herb photographs: Lalithamba, Forest & Kim Starr, Salil Kumar Mukherjee and
> David Monniaux, via Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 2.0 / CC BY 3.0 / CC BY-SA 3.0 /
> CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Swapping in your own photos

Drop your own square JPEG in this folder using the same file name (480x480 or
larger - a crop from your product shoot works well). No code change is needed, and
the credit above can shrink as soon as the licensed photos are gone.
